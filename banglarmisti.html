Banglar Misti:Engineering a Fast & Reliable eCommerce Store

CLIENT:Banglar Misti FIELD:Ecommerce

Designed for modern digital growth—fast, scalable, and optimized to rank higher and convert better.

Their Story

We created a clean, user-friendly, and fully responsive website with seamless navigation and clear product journeys. Additionally, we implemented eCommerce Search Engine Optimization (SEO) to improve visibility, drive organic traffic, and boost online sales.

Bringing Kolkata’s Sweet Magic to Every
Corner of India.

Where They Struggled

The old Banglar Misti website lacked clarity and good user experience. Customers found it difficult to browse, interact, or complete purchases—resulting in low conversions and missed sales opportunities, despite the strong appeal of their Kolkata-famous sweets.

Our Approach

We designed a clean, responsive, and easy-to-use website that beautifully showcases Kolkata’s iconic sweets. Smooth navigation and simple product paths ensure customers across India can explore, choose, and enjoy authentic Kolkata flavours delivered straight to their homes.
