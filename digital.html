Frequently Asked Questions

Digital marketing is the promotion of products or services using digital channels such as search engines, social media, email, and websites to reach and engage a target audience.

SEO improves a website’s visibility on search engines, drives organic traffic, builds credibility, and increases the chances of converting visitors into customers.

Organic marketing focuses on free strategies like SEO and content marketing to attract audiences naturally, while paid marketing involves paid campaigns like Google Ads or social media ads to reach targeted audiences quickly.

Social media platforms help businesses build brand awareness, engage with customers, run targeted ads, and drive traffic to websites or online stores

Success is measured using metrics such as website traffic, conversion rates, click-through rates (CTR), return on investment (ROI), engagement rate, and lead generation.

Content marketing focuses on creating and sharing valuable content (blogs, videos, infographics) to attract, educate, and engage a target audience.
